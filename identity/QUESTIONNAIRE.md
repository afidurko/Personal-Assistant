# Identity & Life Questionnaire

Fill this in over time. Partial answers are fine — mark unknowns as `TODO`.
Every answer becomes mesh memory (`mesh/prefs`, `mesh/people`, `mesh/facts`)
and steers how the assistant team behaves.

**How to answer:** reply in chat section-by-section, or edit this file and
commit. Prefer concrete examples over slogans.

---

## 0. Consent & control (answer first)

1. What may the assistant do **without asking**?
2. What must **always** require your explicit approval?
3. May it wake you (call / FaceTime / text) — if so, for which severity only?
4. Quiet hours? Timezone? Days you never want contact?
5. Kill-switch preference: pause all agents, or only outbound actions?
6. Who else (if anyone) may give the assistant orders about your life?

## Scoring self-test (optional but useful)

For each statement, score 1–5 (disagree → agree):

- I want proactive help, not only reactive answers.
- I would rather approve 10 small actions than 1 large unsupervised one.
- Source quality matters more than speed.
- I am actively looking to change jobs within 6 months.
- Voice/FaceTime escalation is valuable for real emergencies only.
- I want the team to push me on goals I set.
- Simplicity beats feature completeness.
- Shared memory across agents is more important than any single smart model.
