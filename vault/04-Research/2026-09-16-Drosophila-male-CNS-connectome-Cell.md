# Research Brief — Drosophila male CNS connectome (Cell 2026)

## Question
What does the Cell paper on the complete male *Drosophila* CNS connectome report about sexual dimorphism?

## Summary
Berg et al. map the **entire male fruit-fly central nervous system** at synaptic resolution and compare it to female connectomes. Sex differences concentrate in **higher brain centers**; sensory/motor periphery is mostly the same. Male-specific wiring forms **hotspots** and **circuit switches** that reroute sensory information into antagonistic pathways for sex-specific behavior.

## Key numbers (Cell published abstract)
- **~166,700** neurons (brain + nerve cord), fully proofread/annotated
- **~11,710** neuron types
- Annotated for **fruitless / doublesex** expression
- Cross-sex types: **8,069 isomorphic**, **138 dimorphic**, **289 male-specific**, **71 female-specific**

(Preprint used slightly lower counts: 166,691 neurons; 11,691 types; 7,205 / 114 / 262 / 69.)

## Main findings
1. First comprehensive male↔female brain comparison at synaptic resolution.
2. Dimorphic / sex-specific cells cluster centrally; periphery largely shared.
3. Male-specific connections organize into hotspots (male-specific neurons or male-specific arbors on shared cells).
4. Dimorphic neurons **reroute** information across sexes (circuit switches / antagonistic control of opposing behaviors).
5. Resource for full sensory→motor circuit analysis of courtship and related behaviors.

## Counter-arguments / risks / limits
- Connectome ≠ full behavior proof; causal validation still needs physiology/genetics (some already in related work).
- Cell HTML was bot-blocked here; numbers above are from PubMed/abstract metadata, not a full PDF parse of every figure.
- Type-matching across sexes can misclassify borderline dimorphism.

## Recommendation
Treat this as a landmark resource paper: use for circuit hypotheses about sex-specific behavior; follow neuPrint / Janelia MaleCNS viewers for interactive exploration if needed next.
